Platform Transaction and Usage Rules

  1. Deposit and withdrawal processing hours The platform processes deposit and withdrawal requests daily from 10:00 to 23:00. Please plan your transactions accordingly.
  2. Bank details for withdrawals When requesting a withdrawal, provide the account holder’s correct name, bank name and account number. Incorrect details may prevent payment from reaching your account.
  3. Related users and commissions Three or more related users must not be in the same place at the same time. If the system detects and confirms this conduct, the platform will classify it as malicious and unlawful commission abuse.
  4. Money laundering and unlawful transfers Using order tasks for any form of money laundering or unlawful transfer of funds is prohibited. The platform will immediately suspend the accounts involved and pursue legal action against those responsible in accordance with applicable law.
  5. Transfers between users Transfers of funds between users are prohibited. The platform will permanently disable the offending user’s account and its linked upstream and downstream accounts.
  6. Confidentiality Disclosure of the platform’s confidential information is prohibited. The platform will pursue legal action against those responsible in accordance with applicable law.
