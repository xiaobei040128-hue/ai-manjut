3. Legal bases

Service-related processing supports performance of our arrangements with you. Security and diagnostics support legitimate interests in preventing abuse and maintaining reliable services, balanced against your rights. Some processing may be required by applicable law. Where consent is the basis, it can be withdrawn. Without information necessary for a feature, we may be unable to provide it.

4. Recipients

Information may be accessed by authorised staff and account-assigned operators within their permissions; hosting and technical providers; Cloudflare for delivery, security and support-email routing; Google/Gmail for forwarded support email; banks or payment providers involved in your transaction; and authorities where legally required or for legal claims. Cookies maintain login sessions. Browser storage saves language preferences and caches static files. Clearing them may sign you out or reset preferences. If non-essential analytics or advertising trackers are introduced, the required information and consent choices must be provided before use.

6. Retention

There is no single retention period for all records. Criteria include ongoing account use, the purpose of each record, outstanding tasks or payments, security investigations, disputes and applicable record-keeping duties. Contact support for a category-specific explanation or a deletion request; lawful exceptions may require some records to remain.

The website uses HTTPS, permission-limited access, password hashing and encryption of identity/bank account numbers. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Contact us for processing locations and applicable safeguards; this policy does not itself establish a transfer safeguard.
